# SmileNation design brief

## Direction
**Editorial warmth with grounded optimism** — a mature nonprofit identity that feels human, credible, and quietly energetic. The existing logo and documentary photography remain the source of truth; the redesign gives them a calm editorial frame instead of decorative effects.

## Design movement
Contemporary nonprofit editorial: generous whitespace, bold display typography, soft paper-like surfaces, deep green authority, and coral moments of action.

## Core principles
- Lead with dignity and agency, not pity.
- Make every section answer “what changes because SmileNation is here?”
- Pair human photography with short, legible copy.
- Use depth through overlap, borders, and tonal shifts rather than gradients or heavy shadows.
- Make giving and volunteering feel clear, immediate, and safe.

## Color philosophy
Deep evergreen is the anchor for trust and continuity. Warm ivory keeps the canvas human and print-like. Coral is reserved for action and hopeful emphasis. Sage provides connective tissue for program surfaces. Contrast stays high for text and keyboard focus.

## Layout paradigm
A responsive editorial grid: asymmetrical hero with a large image field, structured section bands, a three-card program system, and a closing action panel. On mobile, the same hierarchy collapses into a single reading column with thumb-friendly controls.

## Signature elements
A small uppercase eyebrow, oversized serif headlines, thin rule lines, numbered impact markers, rounded image corners, and a “smile / hope” coral accent line that recurs as a quiet signature.

## Interaction philosophy
Calm, purposeful, and discoverable. Navigation is a compact desktop rail with a clear mobile menu. Cards lift subtly on hover; links show directional movement; focus rings are always visible. No interaction is required to understand the core story.

## Animation
Use restrained CSS transitions only: menu reveal, button lift, card elevation, and a gentle hero image drift. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography system
- Display: `DM Serif Display`, for warmth and editorial confidence.
- UI/body: `Manrope`, for crisp, accessible reading and modern controls.
- Metadata: uppercase `Manrope` with generous tracking.

## Brand essence and voice
SmileNation is warm, practical, hopeful, and community-rooted. Voice is direct and specific: “support education,” “equip children,” “join the work.” Avoid abstract charity language.

## Wordmark/logo
Use the supplied SmileNation logo in the header and footer, with a text fallback only where needed for small screens. Keep its original visual identity intact.

## Signature brand color
Evergreen `#123d35`.

## Asset decisions
Use the supplied `images/logo.png`, `images/banner 1.jpg`, `images/child 1.jpg`, `images/child 2.jpg`, `images/reach 14.jpg`, `images/equip 1.jpg`, and `images/support.png` for concrete placements. The existing photography is more authentic to this organization than newly generated imagery, so no new hero art is introduced.
